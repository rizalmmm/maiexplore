About Our Sea

Diving Komodo

Komodo National Park sits in the Coral Triangle, where the Indian and Pacific Oceans meet. Nutrient-rich currents bring manta rays, reef sharks and large schools of pelagic fish, and the water is exceptionally clear.

Manta Point

Karang Makassar, famous for manta ray encounters.

Castle Rock

Big schools of fish and pelagic action.

Crystal Rock

Crystal-clear water and vibrant reef life.

Batu Bolong

Colourful coral and dense marine life.
