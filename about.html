About Us

Discovering Marine Wonders

Welcome to a world of underwater wonder!

We were born out of a deep love for the ocean and the breathtaking beauty of Indonesia's marine realm. More than just a dive service provider, we are a home for adventurers, nature lovers, and anyone seeking true freedom beneath the waves.

Safety comes first. Every dive is guided by experienced instructors and divemasters who hold high safety standards, so you can enjoy the beauty of the sea with total peace of mind.

Where We're Heading

Our Vision

01

To be a pioneer of sustainable underwater adventures, where every dive inspires love and real action for the conservation of the marine world.

02

To become a trusted marine adventure hub that unifies world-class diving experiences with education and conservation action, preserving the integrity and biodiversity of Indonesia's marine life for future generations.

How We Get There

Our Mission

To realise our vision as a pioneer of sustainable underwater adventures, we are committed to:

01

Safe & Responsible Diving Experiences

Running high-quality daily trips with safety standards as our main focus, and applying eco-friendly diving principles (no-touch policy and strict buoyancy control) on every dive.

02

Educating & Inspiring Divers

Raising awareness among our guests and the community of why marine ecosystems need protecting, through marine life education, ethical photography workshops, and insights into marine fauna behaviour.

03

Eco-Friendly, Zero-Waste Operations

Reducing our carbon footprint and plastic waste across all boat and dive centre operations, including campaigns against single-use plastics.

Our Project

Adventure-Based Education

We believe that protecting the ocean starts with recognising and admiring its life.

  • Our actionUnderwater photography workshops and educational sessions on identifying unique marine life (macro and pelagic species), guided by ethical diving practices and a no-touch policy.
  • Our impactA community of environmentally conscious, responsible divers who care deeply about how marine life behaves.
