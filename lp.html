Chapter 02

Where Two Oceans Collide

Komodo National Park lies inside the Coral Triangle, the meeting point of the Indian and Pacific Oceans. Powerful currents carry nutrients through these waters, which is why Komodo is one of the most iconic dive destinations in the world.

Those same currents draw in the big ones: manta rays, reef sharks and huge schools of pelagic fish. And the visibility is crystal-clear, so you'll see all of it.

Chapter 03

Your Dive Day

Here's how your day with us unfolds.

  1. Step aboard

    Climb onto our daily dive boat, custom-built for divers so the ride out to the dive sites is relaxed and enjoyable.

  2. Gear up with confidence

    Use our high-quality rental gear, maintained regularly for your maximum safety. Bring your own, or don't worry about it.

  3. Meet your guide

    Every dive is led by experienced instructors and divemasters who hold high safety standards. Safety comes first, so you can relax and enjoy the sea with total peace of mind.

  4. Let the current carry you

    Komodo has moderate to strong currents, which make it ideal for drift diving. Relax, stay neutral, and let the reef scroll past you like a film, with the camera ready for underwater landscape shots.

  5. Surface with a story

    Back on the boat with salt in your hair and a story you'll be telling for years. Dive today, memories forever.
