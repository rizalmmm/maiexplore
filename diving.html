About Our Sea

Where Two Oceans Meet

Komodo National Park is one of the most iconic diving destinations in the world. It lies within the Coral Triangle, where the Indian and Pacific Oceans meet, which makes for a rich and dynamic marine ecosystem.

Current phenomena & underwater biodiversity. Strong ocean currents carry plenty of nutrients into these waters, so the coral reefs here thrive.

Top Attraction

Nutrient-rich currents converge here and draw in manta rays, reef sharks and large schools of pelagic fish.

Characteristics

Moderate to strong currents, which make it ideal for drift diving and underwater landscape photography.

Visibility

Crystal-clear visibility and exceptionally high marine biodiversity.

FAQ

Good to Know

Do I need to stay overnight on a boat?

No. Our daily dive trips take you to Komodo's best dive sites and back in a single day, so you don't need a liveaboard.

What marine life can I see in Komodo?

Komodo's nutrient-rich currents attract manta rays, reef sharks and large schools of pelagic fish, as well as healthy coral reefs and macro life.

What are the diving conditions like?

Expect crystal-clear visibility with moderate to strong currents, which are ideal for drift diving and underwater landscape photography.

Can I rent diving equipment?

Yes. We provide high-quality rental gear that is maintained regularly for your safety.
